# SLua Practice Toolbox

Welcome to the practice corner.

These are small, friendly experiments you can rez and play with.
They are **not** a replacement for the live Builders Brewery SLua classes.

SLua is based on **Luau**.
That's the language, not the feast. Bring scripts, not pineapples.

## How we use this toolbox

1. Rez it in the **Builders Brewery Sandbox** (SLua-enabled).
2. Play with it. Break it. Change one thing and see what happens.
3. Bring questions to class, study group, Discord, or group chat.
4. Repeat. That is how it becomes easy.
