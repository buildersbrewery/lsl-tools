# Preparing for AI in Second Life & Content Creation

**Purpose**  
Help Builders Brewery and its students stay effective as AI tools become more common in virtual-world creation workflows.

---

## Current Reality (2026)

- Linden Lab is modernizing scripting with **SLua** (Lua-based). This makes Second Life more familiar to developers who already know Lua from other platforms.
- External AI tools (code assistants, image generators, mesh helpers, etc.) are already used by many creators for:
  - Explaining error messages
  - Generating first-draft scripts or examples
  - Writing documentation and comments
  - Concepting designs
- AI does **not** replace understanding. It amplifies people who already know what good content looks like and how Second Life actually behaves.

---

## What Builders Brewery Should Emphasize

1. **Fundamentals still come first**  
   Events, permissions, communications, and performance limits remain essential. AI-generated scripts that ignore these will still fail or frustrate users.

2. **Critical evaluation**  
   Teach students to test AI suggestions thoroughly in-world and to understand *why* something works or breaks.

3. **SLua readiness**  
   Because SLua is closer to languages many AI models know well, creators who learn its patterns will get better assistance from AI tools in the future.

4. **Human teaching stays central**  
   AI can draft; teachers and mentors help residents turn drafts into reliable, user-friendly products and experiences.

5. **Privacy & ethics**  
   Never paste private resident data, group chat, or personal information into public AI tools. Keep support conversations protected.
